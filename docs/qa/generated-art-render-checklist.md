# Generated art Render checklist

Do not mark visual approval complete until the deployed Render service has been inspected.

- [ ] Build log shows deterministic runtime-art generation before Vite and source cleanup afterward.
- [ ] Every `production-art` request returns a real PNG (no 404 and no LFS pointer response).
- [ ] Alan, Giovanna, Nahir, Damián, Celestino, Hernán, Yamil, Lorena and Selene are visually distinct.
- [ ] Guard, civil and advanced infected use their own sheets and animate.
- [ ] Actor feet and shadows meet the floor; props neither float nor sink.
- [ ] The active weapon is held and only a real inactive weapon is holstered.
- [ ] Protagonist/party HUD and the top-right minimap do not overlap.
- [ ] Objective toast fades away, completion lasts two seconds, and interaction/timer chips remain separate.
- [ ] No permanent control-help card or `H` shortcut appears; all text remains legible.
- [ ] Menu, campaign progression, dialogue, cinematics and the canonical 35 nodes remain intact.

This repository-level checklist records required manual validation, not an assertion that the
deployed visuals have already been approved.
